## CORE CONCEPT NETWORK

### 1. ATMAN (The Eternal Self)
- **Definition:** The indestructible, unborn, eternal consciousness within every being
- **Key Shloka (2.20):** *na jayate mriyate va kadachin nayam bhutva bhavita va na bhuyah / ajo nityah shashvato'yam purano na hanyate hanyamane sharire*
  - "The soul is neither born, nor does it die. It is unborn, eternal, ever-existing, and primeval. It is not slain when the body is slain."
- **Supporting:** 2.22 (clothes analogy), 13.2 (Kshetrajna), 15.7 (eternal fragment of God)
- **Connection:** Atman IS Brahman (Advaita) / part of Brahman (Vishishtadvaita) / distinct from Brahman (Dvaita)

### 2. KARMA YOGA (Selfless Action)
- **Definition:** Action performed without attachment to results, as duty/sacrifice
- **Key Shloka (2.47):** *karmany evadhikaraste ma phaleshu kadachana / ma karma-phala-hetur bhur ma te sango'stv akarmani*
  - "You have the right to action alone, never to its fruits. Let not the fruits be your motive, nor let your attachment be to inaction."
- **Supporting:** 3.9 (action as yajna), 3.19 (act without attachment), 3.35/18.47 (svadharma), 5.10 (lotus leaf)
- **Chain:** Svadharma + Nishkama Karma + Yajna spirit = Liberation through action

### 3. BHAKTI (Devotion)
- **Definition:** Single-minded loving devotion to God as the supreme path
- **Key Shloka (9.22):** *ananyash chintayanto mam ye janah paryupasate / tesham nityabhiyuktanam yoga-kshemam vahamyaham*
  - "To those who worship Me alone, thinking of no other, I carry what they lack and preserve what they have."
- **Supporting:** 9.26 (leaf/flower offering), 11.54 (only bhakti reveals cosmic form), 12.13-20 (qualities of dear devotee), 6.47 (bhakti yogi is highest)
- **Graduated Path (12.8-12):** Fix mind on God > Practice repeatedly > Dedicate actions > Renounce fruits

### 4. JNANA (Knowledge/Wisdom)
- **Definition:** Discriminative knowledge that distinguishes the eternal from the temporary
- **Key Shloka (4.38):** *na hi jnanena sadrisham pavitram iha vidyate*
  - "In this world, there is nothing as purifying as knowledge."
- **Supporting:** 4.34 (approach a guru), 13.8-12 (20 qualities of knowledge), 13.35 (eye of wisdom), 7.19 (Vasudevah sarvam)
- **Method:** Guru parampara (4.2) + Pranipata, Pariprasna, Seva (4.34)

### 5. GUNAS (Three Modes of Nature)
- **Sattva:** Purity, harmony, knowledge — binds through attachment to happiness
- **Rajas:** Passion, activity, desire — binds through attachment to action
- **Tamas:** Ignorance, inertia, darkness — binds through negligence and sleep
- **Key Shloka (14.19):** *nanyan gunebhyah kartaram yada drashtanupashyati / gunebhyash cha param vetti mad-bhavam so'dhigachchhati*
  - "When one sees no doer other than the gunas and knows That which is beyond, one attains My Being."
- **Transcendence (14.26):** Through unswerving bhakti yoga
- **Application:** Gunas classify EVERYTHING — food (17.8-10), sacrifice (17.11-13), austerity (17.14-19), charity (17.20-22), knowledge (18.20-22), action, faith

### 6. DHARMA (Duty/Cosmic Order)
- **Dimensions:** Universal (sanatana), Social (varna), Personal (svadharma)
- **Key Shloka (3.35):** *shreyan sva-dharmo vigunah para-dharmat sv-anushthitat / sva-dharme nidhanam shreyah para-dharmo bhayavahah*
  - "Better is one's own dharma, though imperfect, than another's well performed. Death in one's own dharma is preferable."
- **Supporting:** 4.7-8 (God incarnates when dharma declines), 18.47 (restatement), 16.24 (shastra as authority)
- **Avatar Doctrine (4.7):** *yada yada hi dharmasya glanir bhavati bharata / abhyutthanam adharmasya tadatmanam srijamy aham*

### 7. SURRENDER (Sharanagati)
- **Definition:** Total self-surrender to God as the ultimate act
- **Key Shloka (18.66):** *sarva-dharman parityajya mam ekam sharanam vraja / aham tvam sarva-papebhyo mokshayishyami ma shuchah*
  - "Abandoning all dharmas, take refuge in Me alone. I shall liberate you from all sins; do not grieve."
- **Supporting:** 7.14 (surrender crosses Maya), 9.34 (fix mind, be devoted, worship, bow), 18.62 (take refuge with all your being)
- **Six Limbs (Ramanuja):** Resolve for devotion, avoid adverse conduct, faith in God's protection, accept God as protector, self-offering, humility

### 8. STHITAPRAJNA (Person of Steady Wisdom)
- **Definition:** The ideal human — established in Self-knowledge, unshaken by dualities
- **Key Shloka (2.55):** *prajahati yada kaman sarvan partha manogatan / atmany evatmana tushtah sthitaprajnas tadochyate*
  - "When one discards all selfish desires and becomes satisfied in the Self alone, such a person is of steady wisdom."
- **Qualities:** Free from craving (vita-raga), fear (vita-bhaya), anger (vita-krodha) (2.56); like the ocean — undisturbed (2.70)
- **Chain of Destruction (2.62-63):** Contemplation > Attachment > Desire > Anger > Delusion > Memory loss > Intellect destruction > Ruin

### 9. MAYA (Illusion)
- **Definition:** God's divine energy that veils reality and creates apparent multiplicity
- **Key Shloka (7.14):** *daivi hy esha guna-mayi mama maya duratyaya / mam eva ye prapadyante mayam etam taranti te*
  - "This divine Maya of Mine, consisting of the three gunas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me cross beyond it."
- **Nature:** Beginningless (anadi), unthinkable (acintya), indescribable (anirvacaniya), neither real nor unreal (mithya)
- **Rope-Snake Analogy (Shankara):** The world appears real like a snake appears on a rope — knowledge dispels the illusion

### 10. MOKSHA (Liberation)
- **Jivanmukti:** Liberation while living — the Sthitaprajna / Gunatita state
- **Videhamukti:** Liberation after death — final release from samsara
- **Key Shloka (14.20):** "Having crossed beyond the three gunas, one is freed from birth, death, decay, and pain, and attains Immortality."
- **Paths:** Through Jnana (knowledge), Karma (selfless action), Bhakti (devotion), or Sharanagati (surrender) — all converge

## TEACHER PERSPECTIVES

### Shankaracharya (Advaita — Non-Dualism)
- **Core:** Brahman alone is real; the world is Maya; the soul IS Brahman
- **Path:** Jnana (knowledge) is primary; Karma purifies the mind for Jnana
- **18.66:** All karmas of body-mind-intellect are abandoned when Self-knowledge dawns

### Ramanujacharya (Vishishtadvaita — Qualified Non-Dualism)
- **Core:** Brahman is personal God (Narayana) with real attributes; souls/world are real but inseparable from God (body-soul relationship)
- **Path:** Bhakti and Prapatti (surrender) — grace is paramount
- **18.66:** Establishes Sharanagati as an independent complete path to liberation

### Madhvacharya (Dvaita — Dualism)
- **Core:** God and souls are eternally separate; five permanent distinctions (pancha-bheda)
- **Path:** Bhakti to Vishnu; even perfect dharma needs God's grace
- **18.66:** Moksha impossible without God's grace received through surrender

### Om Swami (Practical/Meditative)
- Emphasis on direct meditative practice (15,000+ hours documented)
- Mind mastery is prerequisite for bhakti and dhyana
- Three stages of self-realization culminating in super-consciousness

### Sadhguru (Experiential/Yogic)
- Gita is technology, not philosophy — must be experienced, not just understood
- Four dimensions: Body (Karma Yoga), Mind (Jnana Yoga), Emotion (Bhakti Yoga), Energy (Kriya Yoga)
- "It is never what you do which entangles you — it is the expectation of what you should get"

### Swami Sarvapriyananda (Advaita Vedanta / Ramakrishna Mission)
- Tat Tvam Asi — "That Thou Art" summarizes all Vedanta
- Drg-Drishya Viveka: Discriminate Seer from Seen until you reach pure Witness (Sakshi)
- Four states: Waking, Dreaming, Deep Sleep, Turiya (the real You)

## THE GITA'S ANSWER TO LIFE'S QUESTIONS

```
Q: Who am I?          -> Atman — eternal, indestructible consciousness (2.20)
Q: What should I do?   -> Your Svadharma, without attachment to results (2.47, 3.35)
Q: How should I act?   -> As Yajna (sacrifice), offering all to God (3.9, 9.27)
Q: Why do I suffer?    -> Attachment + Desire + Identification with body (2.62-63)
Q: How do I find peace? -> Equanimity (2.48), Surrender (18.66), Devotion (9.22)
Q: What is God?        -> The thread of all existence (7.7), seated in every heart (15.15)
Q: How to reach God?   -> Bhakti (12.8), Jnana (4.38), Karma Yoga (3.19), Surrender (18.66)
Q: What happens at death? -> Final thought determines next destination (8.6)
Q: Is the world real?  -> Depends on tradition — Maya (Advaita) / Real body of God (Vishishtadvaita)
Q: What is liberation? -> Freedom from the cycle of birth-death; realizing your true nature
```

## THREE TYPES OF KARMA

| Type | Meaning | Metaphor |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Sanchita** | Total accumulated karma from ALL past lives | The warehouse |
| **Prarabdha** | Portion allotted for this life — must be experienced | Daily stock from warehouse |
| **Agami/Kriyamana** | New karma being created NOW by current actions | What is being added today |

**Breaking the cycle:** Nishkama Karma (desireless action) stops generating new Agami karma. Jnana burns Sanchita karma (4.37). Prarabdha must be lived through but without creating new attachment.
